
## Problem (short definition)
---

To find an optimal policy for USDc liquidity allocation/extraction across three Morpho Blue lending pools:

- USDC/cbBTC : 
- USDC/wstETH : 
- USDC/PT-sUSDE : 

This by only conducting lending actions without borrowing actions.

## Modeling considerations
---

- Interest rate modeling : Morpho's AdaptiveCurveIRM and other related considerations.
- Competitors modeling : Given the game theoretic nature of the protocol.
- Risks modeling : Collateral, Liquidity, Interest Rate related risks.

## Solution
---

Considering the pools’ interest rate models and utilization rates, come up with a system for determining the risk-adjusted optimal distribution ratio of your vault’s USDC into these three lending pools that maximizes the expected lender yield.





# Morpho blue
---

Adaptive Curve IRM

Supply APY

$$
supply_{APY} = borrow_{APY} \cdot R_{u} \cdot (1 - fee)
$$

where: 

- $borrow_{APY} = e^{(borrow_{Rate} \ \times \ secondsPerYear)} - 1$
- $supply_{apy} = borrow_{APY} \times utilization \times (1 - fee)$
- $utilization = \frac{borrowed}{supplied}$
